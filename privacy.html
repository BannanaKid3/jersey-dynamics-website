PROJECT NOTE / PRIVACY

Privacy.

Jersey Dynamics uses contributor information only to organize participation, communicate project updates, and understand where interest is coming from.

What we collect

Our interest form may collect your name, email, broad location, interests, availability, experience, optional notes, participation path, referral source, and email consent.

How we use it

We use this information for contributor onboarding, leadership and partnership follow-up, project communication, and basic aggregate outreach analysis. We do not sell contributor information.

Email

Project-update emails should include an unsubscribe option. Questions can be sent to jerseydynamicsrobotics@gmail.com.

Young contributors

The public intake flow is intended for people age 13 or older. Jersey Dynamics is a student-led open-source project; submitting the form does not create employment, compensation, equity, contracting, or immigration sponsorship.

Questions

Contact jerseydynamicsrobotics@gmail.com to ask about, correct, or request deletion of your submission.
